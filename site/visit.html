GREETING MESSAGE

From Our Pastors

Vincent & Adunola Bello

Jesus only is our message! It’s always a real joy for us to meet and have new members in our church family. We aim to take a practical and engaging approach in reaching out with the gospel of Jesus Christ and helping people along the journey to Heaven. We stress cultural relevancy and utilize all means to simply communicate gospel of Eternal Life through Jesus Christ alone.

We serve the city of Harrisburg, surrounding communities and our members through the true and undiluted Word of God. We value fellowship and building deep relationships within the community. We are not only interested in supporting your spiritual growth but also joining hands to support your physical needs: “Pure religion and undefiled before God and the Father is this, To visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction, and to keep himself unspotted from the world.” James 1:27.

We welcome you! All are welcome!
